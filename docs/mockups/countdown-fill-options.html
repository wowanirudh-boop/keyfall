What I'd pick

Countdown fill, on its own. It encodes order and timing in one continuous signal — the fullest key is next, and how full it is tells you how long you have. It degrades honestly on chords, because simultaneous notes fill at the same rate and therefore look identical, which is true.

Ordinal numbers are the clearest for a beginner but add a second thing to read while your hands are busy, and they churn constantly. Fade-by-imminence is elegant and costs nothing, but four levels of dimness is near the limit of what's distinguishable in peripheral vision. Next-only is unambiguous and throws away the preparation time the lead exists to give you.

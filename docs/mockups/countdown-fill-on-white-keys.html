D-022 countdown fill · after D-047 · nothing decided

The falling-key fill lost its colour. It doesn’t have to.

The fill shows how soon a key is due — it rises as the note approaches. It used to be the hand colour, which worked because the key underneath was almost black. Now the key is almost white, and the hand colour on white is 1.79:1 — near-invisible. D-047 solved that by going near-black. The third option keeps the hue and darkens it instead.

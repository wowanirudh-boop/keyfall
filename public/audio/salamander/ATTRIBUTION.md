# Salamander Grand Piano sample subset

- Instrument and recordings: Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
- Source instrument: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
- MP3 distribution source: https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/legalcode

This self-hosted subset contains 15 root samples spaced six semitones apart,
plus the C8 endpoint. The MP3 files total 1,036,423 bytes (0.99 MiB), measured
on 2026-08-11. The D-008 cap is 8,388,608 bytes (8 MiB).
